About the centre

An interdisciplinary initiative across ten departments

The Centre for Complex Systems & Dynamics is a multi-disciplinary initiative started by faculty from across ten departments at IIT Madras. It provides a common platform for researchers from science, engineering and the humanities to develop new techniques and tools for modelling and analysing complex dynamical systems.

Beyond advancing the fundamental understanding of the universal features shared across such systems, the centre focuses on translating that understanding into technology useful in industrial and societal contexts.

Our mission

To provide a common platform where researchers from different disciplines develop new mathematical tools for modelling and analysing complex dynamical systems — and translate them into real-world technology.

Our vision

To enhance the fundamental understanding of the universal features underlying similar phenomena across a diversity of systems — from climate and neuroscience to active flows.

“Problems that defy a classical first-principles approach, involve many interacting degrees of freedom, and demand large datasets in a physics-informed framework — that is where our work begins.”

Thrust research directions

Six interdisciplinary verticals

Each vertical is interdisciplinary in nature, drawing expertise from varied disciplines to tackle problems that resist classical modelling, involve many interacting parts, or require physics-informed learning from large datasets.

Complex networks

Structure, dynamics and resilience of large interacting networks — from collaboration graphs to climate and epidemics.

Nonlinear dynamics

Flows, bifurcations, limit cycles and chaos — and the transitions between dynamical states in engineered and natural systems.

Stochastic dynamics

The role of noise and randomness — noise-induced transitions, stochastic resonance and uncertainty in dynamical systems.

Biocomplexities & biomimetic flows

Living and active matter, collective behaviour, and flows inspired by biology across many length scales.

Data science & causal discovery

Learning causal network models from data, combining machine learning with the first principles of physics.

Scientific machine learning

Physics-informed and data-driven learning — surrogate models, operator learning and hybrid schemes that fuse first-principles physics with modern ML.
