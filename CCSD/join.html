Join us

Come do research with us

We are always looking for curious, self-driven people who want to understand how complex, organised behaviour emerges from many interacting parts. Whether you are an undergraduate, a prospective graduate student, or a researcher, there is likely a way to work with the centre.

What we look for

The kind of researcher who thrives here

Our research sits at the meeting point of mathematics, physics, computation and engineering. The most important thing is not a specific background, but genuine curiosity about complex systems and a willingness to learn the tools — analytical, computational and experimental — needed to study them.

We value people who enjoy working across disciplinary boundaries, who are comfortable being wrong on the way to being right, and who like building things — models, code, experiments and ideas — together with others.

Curiosity first

A fascination with patterns, emergence and the behaviour of systems with many parts.

Quantitative skills

Comfort with mathematics and programming — or the drive to get there quickly.

Collaborative spirit

A liking for interdisciplinary teamwork across departments and research groups.

Persistence

The patience to wrestle with hard, open-ended problems over months and years.

PhD students

Doctoral research

For those ready to commit to a deep, multi-year research problem.

PhD students are the engine of the centre's research. You will join one of our faculty groups and work on an open problem in complex systems — developing theory, building models and computational tools, and publishing your results. Admissions are through the respective departments and the Institute's research-admission process.

  • A Master's or strong Bachelor's degree in engineering, science or mathematics.
  • Identify faculty whose work excites you and write to them with your interests and CV.

Master's & IDDD

Graduate & dual-degree study

Structured programmes with a strong research project component.

IIT Madras undergraduates can specialise through the Interdisciplinary Dual Degree (IDDD) in Complex Systems & Dynamics. Students from around the world can join the International Interdisciplinary M.Tech (I2MP). Both combine coursework in networks, nonlinear dynamics and computation with a substantial research project supervised by centre faculty.

Interns & undergrads

Project & summer internships

A first taste of research in complex systems.

We host motivated undergraduate and early-stage students for project and summer internships. These are a chance to pick up the tools of the trade — dynamical-systems theory, network science, scientific computing — while contributing to a live research project. When writing, tell us what you have worked on, what you would like to learn, and the dates you are available.

Postdocs

Postdoctoral research

For researchers ready to lead their own line of work.

Postdoctoral researchers bring new expertise and help shape the centre's direction. IIT Madras runs institute postdoctoral and young-faculty fellowship schemes, and individual faculty often have funded positions. If your research connects to our themes, write to the faculty member whose work is closest to yours, with a CV and a short statement of research interests.

How to reach us

Writing a good first email

A short, specific email goes a long way. Tell us who you are, what you have worked on, which of our themes or faculty interest you and why, and attach a CV. Mentioning a paper or project of ours that caught your attention helps us point you to the right person.
