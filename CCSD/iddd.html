About the programme

Model the complex, across disciplines

The IDDD in Complex Systems & Dynamics introduces students to the techniques used to model complex systems and to investigate challenging dynamical problems in climate science, neuroscience, biological systems and beyond. It builds an appreciation of the universal features that recur across seemingly different systems, and equips students with the mathematical and computational tools to study them.

Students earn the dual degree alongside their core B.Tech discipline, combining coursework in complex networks, nonlinear dynamics and numerical methods with a year-long research project supervised by centre faculty.

Who can apply

Open to all IIT Madras B.Tech students, any stream.

Eligibility

CGPA above 8.0 at the end of Semester 5.

Electives

45 credits from approved baskets.

Project

A one-year dual-degree research project.

What the programme develops

Skills you build

Mathematical modelling

Formulating and analysing models of systems with many interacting degrees of freedom.

Dynamical-systems thinking

Stability, bifurcations, chaos and the universal features that recur across domains.

Network science

Representing and analysing interacting systems as complex networks.

Scientific computing

Numerical methods and simulation for large, nonlinear and stochastic models.

Data-driven methods

Combining data and machine learning with the first principles of physics.

Research practice

An extended, supervised research project from question to results.

Curriculum

Structure of the degree

The programme comprises three core courses, a basket of electives totalling 45 credits, and a year-long research project.

Core courses

Three required courses, to be completed by Semester 8 — one from each group below.

Course Code Typically offered
Complex networksID5080Odd semesters
Nonlinear dynamicsPH5500Even semesters
Mathematics & numerical techniquesAM5650Flexible

Electives · 45 credits

Five courses of 9 credits each, or a combination of four higher-credit courses, drawn from approved baskets including data science, high-performance computing and engineering specialisations relevant to complex systems.

  • Data science & machine learning
  • High-performance & scientific computing
  • Domain electives — fluid dynamics, networks, neuroscience, control

Dual-degree project

A one-year research project, with topics set in consultation with faculty advisors at the centre. The project is the heart of the dual degree — an extended, original investigation that often leads to a publication.

Course codes and offerings are indicative and may be revised each year. For the current, authoritative curriculum and course lists, see the SIDIS IDDD page or write to the centre.
