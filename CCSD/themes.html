Research

Research Themes

Our work spans six interdisciplinary themes, each drawing expertise from varied disciplines to tackle problems that resist classical modelling, involve many interacting parts, or require physics-informed learning from large datasets.

See ongoing projects Meet the people

Complex networks

Structure, dynamics and resilience of large interacting networks — from collaboration graphs to climate and epidemics.

Nonlinear dynamics

Flows, bifurcations, limit cycles, chaos and turbulence — and the transitions between dynamical states in engineered and natural systems.

Stochastic dynamics

The role of noise and randomness — noise-induced transitions, stochastic resonance and uncertainty in dynamical systems.

Complexity & Optimisation

Emergence from many interacting units, and the optimisation and control problems that arise in complex, high-dimensional systems.

Biocomplexities & biomimetic flows

Living and active matter, collective behaviour, and flows inspired by biology across many length scales.

Data science & scientific ML

Causal discovery and physics-informed learning — surrogate models, operator learning and hybrid schemes fusing physics with modern ML.

From themes to projects

Each theme is realised in concrete, ongoing projects — from social-media dynamics and climate networks to bio-mimetic flows and causal discovery in industrial processes.
