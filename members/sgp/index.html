S Ganga Prasath

Assistant Professor, IIT Madras

I am currently a faculty member in the Department of Applied Mechanics & Biomedical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras. My research interests lie at the interface of scientific ML, inverse problems, robotics & control, and soft-mechanics.

I am also the faculty advisor of the Applied Mechanics Scholars Association and help the enthusiastic scholars community run the seminar series 42 Explained! and ChalkTalk. Outside work, I have a set of trinkets that keep me busy. You can access their progress using the links below. Write to me if you are interested in collaborating or would like to know more about any of these topics.
